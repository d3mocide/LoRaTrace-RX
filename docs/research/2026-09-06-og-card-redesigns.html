Redesigning the three original cards

Activity and Radio now share a design language: a visual band across the top, three statCard()s under it, with the card owning a question and Enter running the verb that answers it. Channel, GPS and System predate that and still read as flat readouts beside them.

Three options each. Green costs nothing but layout work; red needs new firmware state, named per option. Every variation uses only data the device can honestly produce — no fabricated series (docs/research/V2_DESIGN.md §3).

How to read the mockups. Each is a real 240×135 layout at 2.2×, drawn with the same geometry the firmware would use: bands are 236×43 at y=14, cards are 77×60 at y=61, and subtitles are clamped to 11 characters like statCard() does. Values are plausible field readings, not live data.

Card 3 · Channel

Owns: am I on the right channel, and what did it yield? Today it is the weakest card on the device — four lines that never change unless you switch profile. Captures, Nodes and Probe are already its views 2–4; the card itself should make the question answerable.

Owns: will the device survive the drive? The densest of the three and the closest to useful already. What it cannot answer is the tense in its own question: every number is instantaneous, so a heap leak or a dying battery looks identical to a healthy one until it isn't.

Picking

CardLowest riskBest answer to the card's questionCost of the bold one
ChannelA — Band positionA. The band map is the answer: if the energy is all somewhere you are not tuned, you see it without reading a number. None. Capture frequencies and sweep peaks are already in RAM.
GPSA — Constellation bandB. A fix that flickers between 3D and 2D while driving is what silently corrupts a run, and only a time series shows it. 30-byte sats ring + one logger counter for untagged rows.
SystemC — Health matrixB. "Will it survive" is a question about a trend; a flat heap line is the only honest way to answer it. 30-byte heap ring. The battery estimate is the part to be careful with.
One caution on System B. "~4h 10m" is a projection, and this project's own boundary is that the UI must not present derived confidence as measurement. If that card ships it should read the measured discharge slope with an explicit basis ("at 30min rate"), or not make the claim at all — the heap sparkline carries the option on its own.
