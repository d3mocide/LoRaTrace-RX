Interpretation:

- The first AP start cost about 56KB while active.
- The cold-to-first-warm off-state difference was 18444B. Inspection of the
  bundled Arduino-ESP32 framework showed one-time TCP/IP event-task and
  network-interface initialization retained for reuse; treat the first warm
  off state as the lifecycle baseline rather than labeling `heap_min` alone
  as a leak.
- Cycle 2 lost another 716B and added 13 allocated blocks. `startAp()` was
  calling `registerRoutes()` on every cycle, while `WebServer::stop()` keeps
  its request-handler list. Route registration was made idempotent after this
  run. Ten fixed-build cycles are required to confirm the diagnosis.
- Browser/download current heap partially recovered from about 170.8KB to
  174.2KB while the client remained associated. A disconnect and AP stop were
  needed for the lifecycle comparison; the full repeated-download matrix was
  not completed.
